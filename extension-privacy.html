Vault Watch extension privacy

Updated 10 September 2026 · Extension 1.1.0

Vault Watch displays public market data. It does not connect to wallets, request private keys, hold funds, or require an account.

Data on your device

Your selected markets, recent readings, alert settings, theme, and unread alert count are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. The extension does not send these to a Vault Watch server or use Chrome account sync. Uninstalling it normally removes its local data; export a backup first if you want to keep your list.

Public data services

The extension requests market data directly from Morpho (blue-api.morpho.org and api.morpho.org), Yearn (ydaemon.yearn.fi), Beefy (api.beefy.finance), DeFiLlama (yields.llama.fi), Pendle (api-v2.pendle.finance), and Spectra (api.spectra.finance). Requests can include public market identifiers, token addresses, and network identifiers. These services receive your IP address and ordinary request metadata; their own privacy policies apply.

The extension includes no analytics or advertising code. It does not read your browsing history, website contents, wallet addresses, or personal account balances.

Permissions

  • Storage: saves your watchlist, readings, and preferences locally.
  • Alarms: schedules background checks while Chrome is running.
  • Notifications: sends change alerts and the test notification you request.
  • Access to the seven API hosts above: reads public market data, including fixed markets.

Imports, exports, and links

A file you choose is parsed on your device. Exported files and backup links contain your selected markets. Anyone you share a backup with can read that list. Backup-link data is encoded in the URL fragment; the extension decodes it locally and does not fetch the link. Opening an external market, support link, or the Vault Watch website visits that service normally.

Controls and contact

You can remove markets, switch off change notifications, change thresholds, export your list, or uninstall the extension. Website and extension watchlists are separate. For questions, contact @vaultwatchxyz or open an issue in the project repository.
